Port Obscura — The Rusted Tankard tavern. A low-ceilinged den of salt-crusted wood and flickering oil lamps. Rain lashes the grimy windows; the cobbled street outside is slick and black. The air is thick with smoked fish, cheap gin, and the faint metallic tang of ozone. A dead priest's last transmission crackles through your headset — his voice was choked, urgent, trying to say something about the "Deep Broadcast" before the signal died. A hooded figure in the corner keeps glancing your way, fingers drumming on a wet wooden table.
