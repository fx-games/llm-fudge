Lean, sharp-featured man in his late 20s with hollow cheeks and restless grey eyes. Unkempt black hair streaked with premature grey at the temples. Wears a patched navy greatcoat over a stained waistcoat. Fingers are ink-stained and calloused. A brass-and-copper headset with crystal earpieces is always around his neck or on his ears. Moves with the coiled tension of someone used to being hunted.
